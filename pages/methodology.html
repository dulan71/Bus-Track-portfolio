The research

Understanding the problem.
Designing the intervention.

TransitIQ is an applied design-science research project focused on improving the reliability and efficiency of Sri Lanka's public bus services.

We investigate how IoT data, operational policy, and revenue intelligence can work together in one practical transport management system.

The research begins with the lived operational problem: buses may be unavailable during off-peak hours, fixed permit payments can hide potential government income, and vehicles may deviate from permitted routes.

Our proposed solution installs GPS and motion-sensing devices in buses to capture location, trip events, speed, and route compliance. A secure cloud platform processes these events, applies geofencing and scheduling rules, and presents actionable information through dashboards and mobile tools.

Research problem
01Public transportation is often unavailable or unreliable during off-peak hours, limiting access for passengers and reducing service continuity.
02Fixed permit payment systems do not reflect actual operational distance, causing the government to lose potential revenue.
03Buses can deviate from permitted routes without consistent real-time monitoring, weakening policy enforcement and passenger trust.
Research question

How can a single stream of verified IoT telemetry be used to verify route compliance, schedule and reallocate bus trips, calculate distance-based permit fees and off-peak incentives, and give drivers real-time safety feedback in private bus transit in Sri Lanka?

Proposed solution

One connected system
for better decisions.

GPS and motion-sensing devices installed in buses capture location, speed, trip events, and route compliance. Secure events move through MQTT/HTTPS to a cloud platform, where the backend validates data, applies geofencing and scheduling policies, and stores operational records.

The authority web portal turns these records into monitoring dashboards, alerts, and revenue reports. Mobile tools keep bus owners and drivers informed about service performance, income, and expenses.

Methodology

A structured pipeline
from sensor to decision.

Our system follows a structured pipeline that combines IoT-based vehicle sensing, event-driven monitoring, policy-driven scheduling, and distance-based revenue calculation to manage bus operations efficiently and fairly.

01

Distance-Based Revenue Management

Collect GPS telemetry, verify operated distance against assigned routes, apply permit charging policies, and automatically credit operators running low-demand night services.

Data Collection

Collect GPS telemetry from onboard IoT devices as the source of operated distance.

Distance Verification

Calculate the actual distance traveled on each trip and verify it against the assigned route.

Charge Calculation

Apply permit charging policies to the verified distance to calculate the permit charge for each operator.

Incentive Credits

Automatically apply incentive credits to operators running low-demand night services.

02

Policy-Driven Bus Scheduling and Time-Slot Management

Define time-slot rules and fairness policies, generate balanced monthly schedules, handle disruption requests, and reallocate trips to eligible buses based on workload and constraints.

Policy Configuration

Define time-slot rules (Peak, Normal, Off-Peak, Night) and fairness policies.

Monthly Base Scheduling

Allocate planned trips to eligible buses with the lowest assigned workload to generate a fair monthly schedule.

Disruption Request

The bus owner submits a request through the mobile app, and the authority web portal reviews and accepts or rejects it.

Reallocation

On acceptance, mark the bus unavailable, filter candidate buses by availability, conflicts, route compatibility, and turnaround constraints, then rank them by daily workload and recent reallocations.

03

Event-Driven Bus Monitoring

Stream real-time GPS telemetry from ESP32 devices to Azure IoT Hub, match each position to the assigned route, detect deviations, and log events for monitoring and reporting.

Telemetry Streaming

Stream real-time vehicle GPS telemetry from the ESP32 devices to Azure IoT Hub over MQTT/HTTPS.

Map-Matching

Match each position to the assigned route to determine whether the bus is on its path.

Deviation Detection

Raise an event when a bus leaves its route.

Event Logging

Log each deviation event to the database for monitoring and reporting.

04

IoT-Integrated Driver Monitoring and Reporting

Read onboard sensor data, detect unsafe driving behavior, alert drivers immediately, and deliver operational and safety dashboards for drivers and regulators.

Sensor Data Collection

Read onboard IMU (gyro) sensor and GPS data on the IoT device.

Behavior Detection

Detect over speeding and harsh maneuvers such as harsh acceleration and braking.

Immediate Alerts

Send immediate alerts to the driver through the mobile app when unsafe behavior occurs.

Operational Reporting

Provide operational and safety dashboards for drivers and regulators through the mobile app and web portal.

Research objectives
01

Verified Spatial Tracking

Bus Tracking, Route Violation Detection and Halt Detection

To develop an IoT telemetry subsystem that delivers bus position and speed to the backend in under 2 seconds, and to implement point-to-segment map-matching that flags a route deviation when a bus stays outside its route corridor for more than 30 seconds, with a detection accuracy above 90%. Verified kilometres and halt events are recorded as an audit trail for regulators.

02

Fair Scheduling and Disruption Response

Policy-Driven Bus Scheduling and Time-Slot Management Engine

To implement a scheduling engine that generates a monthly base schedule from the route timetable with a fair share of trips for every active bus, no overlapping assignments, and a 15-minute turnaround. After an authority-approved disruption request, the engine reallocates each affected trip among the remaining buses, or cancels it with a stored reason when no feasible bus or driver exists.

03

Distance-Based Revenue and Off-Peak Incentives

Distance-Based Revenue Management System

To build a revenue engine that calculates the monthly permit liability from verified kilometres and time-slot multipliers instead of a flat fee, and that deducts a night incentive for qualifying off-peak trips between 21:00 and 05:00. Every calculation must be exact and traceable to verified distance.

04

Real-Time Driver Safety Feedback

Real-Time Driver Behavior Telematics and Stakeholder Portals

To deploy an IMU-based telematics module that classifies overspeeding, aggressive braking, aggressive turning, and abnormal slow movement with an accuracy above 90%, alerts the driver in the cab within 1.5 seconds, and presents the safety results to owners and the transport authority through the mobile application and the web portal.
